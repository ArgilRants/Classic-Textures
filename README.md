# Classic Textures
![Mod Icon made by zebragoboom](/readme_assets//icon.webp "Mod Icon made by zebragoboom")

Classic Textures is a Balatro mod that, with help from [Malverk](https://github.com/Eremel/Malverk), adds two texture packs with the aim to bring to bring the old, unseen and classicsprites back to the game.

![Page 1 jokers with changed joker pack, and star stake stickers pack](/readme_assets//example.png "Page 1 jokers with changed joker pack, and star stake stickers pack")
